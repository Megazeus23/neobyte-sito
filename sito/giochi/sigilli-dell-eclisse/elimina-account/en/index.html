Deleting your account

Seals of the Eclipse, developed by NeoByte Studios.

From the game

  1. Open the game and tap your name at the top left of the main screen.
  2. Open the Help tab.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Type the confirmation word the game asks for and tap the button to delete the account forever.

The steps are the same on Android and on iPhone. Deletion is immediate and final: the game cannot be recovered. All open sessions are closed.

The same steps also apply if you play as a guest. As a guest, without a linked Google account, logging out also deletes the game, like Delete account: the game reminds you before you log out.

If you link Google from your profile and that Google account already has its own game, the game asks you which one to keep: the other is deleted forever, as with Delete account.

Without the game

If you no longer have the game installed, write to privacy@neobytestudios.com from the email address of your Google or Apple account, with the subject “Account deletion Seals of the Eclipse”. We carry out the request only if it comes from the same email address you use to sign in to the game, and we delete the account within 30 days, with a confirmation reply. If you chose to hide your email with Apple, delete the account from the game as explained above.

If you play as a guest we do not have your email: delete the account from the game as explained above. If you have already uninstalled the game, cleared its data or lost your phone without having linked Google, the game can no longer be recovered: nobody can get back into it, and without an email we cannot recognise it as yours. A guest game without a linked Google account and without sign-ins for 12 months is deleted automatically, as with Delete account.

What is deleted and what remains

  • Deleted immediately: the link with your Google or Apple account and its email (as a guest, the fingerprint of the code to get back in), your player name, progress, cards, resources, missions and sessions.
  • Public profile, Arena and Bosses: we immediately delete the profile other players see (name, portrait, frame, title, level and code), your place and formation in the Arena with your battles and replays, and your damage to shared Bosses: you disappear from all leaderboards. The Bosses you started stay open for the others, without your name.
  • Chat: we immediately delete the messages you wrote in the global chat, including the announcements of your summons, and your private chats, with the messages you wrote and those you received.
  • Mail: your Mail is deleted immediately. The messages you wrote to other players stay in their Mail until they delete them, but without your name: in place of the sender a label says that the player is no longer in the game.
  • Reports and blocks: we immediately delete the reports made about you, with the copies of your messages and of your name, the moderation actions on your account, your blocks and the community rules you accepted. Suspensions of messages or of the account still in force, however, stay tied to the fingerprint of the phones you played on, with the type of block, the reason, the expiry and the date but without the link to your account, and apply to anyone playing on those phones until the block expires or is lifted (with a permanent suspension, as long as the game is active): this way a block cannot be avoided with a new account. The reports you made about others, with the copy of the reported message or name, stay without the link to you until we check them; after the decision they are deleted within 30 days.
  • Backups: the data also disappears from the database backups within 30 days.
  • Purchases: only an irreversible fingerprint of the purchase code remains, with no link to you, to prevent the same purchase from being redeemed twice. Gems bought and not spent are lost with the account. The fingerprint stays as long as the game is active. We do not keep players’ tax data: payments are collected by Google or Apple, which issue the receipts.
  • Server technical logs (IP address, time, requested page): they delete themselves after 14 days.

Uninstalling the app does not delete the account: the game stays on the server. With Google or Apple you find it again by signing in; as a guest, without a linked Google account, you can no longer get back in, and after 12 months without sign-ins the game deletes itself.
