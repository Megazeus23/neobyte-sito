  • Gems are not money. Chat, Mail and community rules

In the global chat you can write to all players; in private chats and in Mail you write to one player at a time. Everyone sees your player name. Before writing you must read and accept the community rules, which apply to the global chat, private chats, Mail messages and player names:

  • Respect people. Threats, harassment, insults, discrimination and incitement to violence are forbidden. Do not stalk other players and do not get around their blocks.
  • Protect privacy. Do not write personal data about yourself or others: addresses, phone numbers, emails, passwords, access codes or information that can identify a person. Messages with links, emails or phone numbers are not sent.
  • Content suitable for everyone. Swear words and sexual, vulgar or illegal content are forbidden, especially if they involve minors. In the chat and in Mail swear words are covered with ***, but they remain forbidden: anyone who keeps it up may be muted. They are not allowed in names.

An automatic filter checks messages and names: in chat and Mail messages it covers swear words with ***, in names it does not allow them. Links, email addresses and phone numbers are allowed neither in messages nor in names. Against spam there are limits on how many messages can be sent each day. You are responsible for the messages you write.

Chat messages may be translated automatically into the reader’s language with the help of an external artificial intelligence service (Anthropic). The translation may contain errors: the original text prevails. Details on data are in the Privacy Policy.
