Privacy Policy

Seals of the Eclipse, a game for Android and iOS. Last updated: 26 November 2026.

Who runs the game

Data controller: Jetmir Lleshi, sole proprietorship trading as NeoByte Studios, Via Guglielmo Marconi 37/A, 39055 Laives (BZ), Italy, VAT number 03297110219, REA number BZ-248476. Privacy contact: privacy@neobytestudios.com.

What data we use

  • Account. You can play as a guest or sign in with Google (on Android and iPhone) or with Apple (on iPhone). With the same Google account you find the same game on Android and on iPhone. With Google or Apple we receive an anonymous identifier of your account, used to find your game, and the account’s email address (if you chose to hide it with Apple, we receive Apple’s relay address). The email is only used to recognise you when you write to support or ask for your account to be deleted: we do not use it for advertising, other players do not see it, and it is deleted together with the account. Google’s sign-in token may also contain the name and photo of your Google profile: the server does not read or keep them. We never receive your password.
  • Guest account. As a guest you give us neither an email nor your real name. When you choose to play as a guest, the server creates the account and gives the phone a secret code to get back in, which stays saved in the app’s data on the phone; the server keeps only an irreversible fingerprint of the code. You can link Google whenever you like: from then on we receive the identifier and email of the Google account as described above, and the guest code stops working. If that Google account already has its own game, you choose which one to keep: the other is deleted as with “Delete account”.
  • Game. The player name you choose, your progress, cards, resources and missions.
  • Public profile. Your player name, a 5-character code, portrait, frame, title, level and rank are visible to other players: in the leaderboards, in the Arena, in shared Bosses, in the chat and in Mail messages. The leaderboards also show your Arena points and the damage you deal to Bosses. For this reason, do not use your real name as your player name.
  • Mail and messages between players. If you write to another player, the message (subject, text, date and who wrote it) stays in the recipient’s Mail until they delete it. An automatic filter checks messages and names: in messages it covers swear words with *** and blocks links, email addresses and phone numbers; in names none of these are allowed. Against spam we keep for 2 days a log of who wrote to whom and when, without the text. Before writing you accept the community rules: we keep which version you accepted and when.
  • Chat. We keep the messages you write in the global chat and in private chats: text, date, who wrote it and, for private chats, to whom. Messages in the global chat are seen by all players, except those who have blocked you, together with your public profile. Private messages are seen only by you and the other player (their text also goes through automatic translation, described below); if one of you reports a message, the staff receives a copy to check it. The automatic filter covers swear words with *** and does not send messages with links, email addresses or phone numbers. When you summon an Ultra Rare or Legendary card with stones, the global chat announces it to everyone with the card’s name and your player name.
  • Automatic chat translation. The chat automatically translates messages into the language of the person reading them. To do this, our server sends the text of the message to an external artificial intelligence provider (Anthropic, with its Claude Haiku model) and receives the translation; the server then checks the result (swear words are covered with ***) before showing it. Only the text of the message goes to the provider: never your name, your player code or number, your email or any other account data. It is used only to translate. Anthropic processes the text outside the European Union as well, for example in the United States (no specific country is stated). For these transfers the European Union’s standard contractual clauses apply, included in our contract with Anthropic. Anthropic deletes the messages from its systems within 30 days; the exceptions are legal obligations and violations of its usage policies, in which case they may be kept longer. Anthropic does not use these messages to train its models. For this processing the controller is NeoByte Studios and Anthropic is the processor. The legal basis is the performance of the service you asked for, because the translated chat is part of the game. The chat is optional: if you do not want a message of yours to be translated, do not write it in the chat.
  • Reports, blocks and actions. If you report a message (in Mail or in the chat), a name or a player, we keep the report with the reason, the note you wrote and a copy of the reported message or name. The list of players you have blocked stays until you unblock them. If you do not follow the community rules, we record the action taken (for example messages suspended, name changed, account suspended) with the reason.
  • Arena and shared Bosses. For the Arena we keep your points, wins, a copy of the teams you signed up with and the log of battles (for rematches and replays). Opponents see only your public profile, points, and the attack and health of your formation, never your cards. For shared Bosses we keep the damage you dealt.
  • Sessions. The type of device (for example “Android phone”), the phone fingerprint (see the next item) and the time of your last sign-in, to keep only one session open per account and to warn you if the account is opened elsewhere. Of the session key the server keeps only an irreversible fingerprint.
  • Phone fingerprint. On Android, at every sign-in the app derives a code on the phone from the Android identifier that the system assigns to the app (it is not the advertising ID). The server does not receive the real identifier but a fingerprint, which the server encrypts again with its own key before saving it with the session. It is used to apply a suspension of messages or of the account to the whole phone, for anyone who plays on it (even with an existing account) until it expires, and to limit how many guest accounts can be created.
  • Google Play check. On Android, when you open the app and when you sign in, the app uses Play Integrity, a Google Play service that checks that the game is the original one downloaded from Google Play and that the phone has not been tampered with. For this the app sends Google data about the app (name, version and signing certificate), about the Google Play licence and about the phone (security certificates of the phone and of Google Play services), together with a one-time check code from our server. Our server receives from Google only the result of the check (for example “app recognised by Google Play” or “phone intact”): it uses it to protect the game from fake apps and cheating, remembers in the session only whether and when the check succeeded, and writes the result in the technical log, which deletes itself within 14 days. Google processes this data under its own rules: it encrypts it, does not give it to others and deletes it after a set period.
  • Purchases. Gems are bought with Google Play on Android and with Apple’s App Store on iPhone. If you buy gems, we receive from the store the product identifier and the purchase code, and we ask the store whether the payment is valid or has been refunded. On Android, at the time of payment we send Google a code derived from your game account, which does not reveal who you are, to link the purchase to the right game. The purchase code stays encrypted on the server only as long as needed to complete the purchase with the store. If a purchase is refunded, its gems are removed from the game. Payment is handled by Google or Apple: we never see credit cards or payment details.
  • IP address. It is used to deliver responses to your device and to limit repeated attempts (against abuse). The limits are kept only in memory; the server records the IP address, time, requested page and technical details of the request (for example the type of app or browser) in technical logs that delete themselves after at most 35 days.
  • Sign-ins from the browser. The game can only be used from the app: you cannot sign in from a browser (these pages remain open to everyone). If someone tries to sign in from a browser with Google or Apple, the sign-in is refused without creating any account, and the server notes in its technical log the identifier and email of the account used, to check refused sign-ins. The log deletes itself within 14 days.

We do not use advertising, profiling, behavioural analytics or the device’s location. We do not sell your personal data and we do not give it to others for advertising. On Android the app asks for these permissions: Internet access (to play online), network state (to know whether the connection is metered before downloading the game’s images), vibration (for the game’s vibration, which you can turn off in the options) and Google Play billing (to buy gems). It does not ask for location, camera, microphone, contacts or access to your files.

Why we use it

To let you play and keep your game (performance of the service you asked for). To deliver and check purchases (performance of the contract and tax obligations). To answer your support and account deletion requests (performance of the service and legal obligations on privacy). To protect the game from abuse and cheating, including with the phone fingerprint that enforces suspensions and limits the creation of guest accounts, with the Google Play check on the app and the phone, and with the log of sign-ins refused from the browser (legitimate interest). To enforce the community rules in the chat, in Mail and in names, with the filter, reports, blocks and actions (performance of the service and legitimate interest in keeping the game safe for everyone). To translate chat messages automatically (performance of the service you asked for, because the translated chat is part of the game).

Where data is kept and who processes it

  • The game server runs on a Hostinger virtual machine with the data in Frankfurt (Germany, European Union).
  • Google (sign-in with Google, Google Play, purchases on Android and the Google Play check) and Apple (sign-in with Apple, App Store and purchases on iPhone) process data under their own privacy policies, also outside the European Union.
  • Anthropic, an external artificial intelligence provider, receives the text of chat messages in order to translate them, as described in the item “Automatic chat translation”; for translation it receives no other data. In addition, the automatic system that watches over the game sends Anthropic some error lines from the technical log (request addresses and error messages, without emails) to find and fix faults. Anthropic acts as processor, processes the text outside the European Union as well, for example in the United States, with the European Union’s standard contractual clauses, and deletes it from its systems within 30 days, except for legal obligations and violations of its usage policies. It does not use the text to train its models.
  • Other players see your public profile, the messages you send them and the ones you write in the global chat.

If you live outside the European Union

The game is published in many countries, including outside the European Union. Wherever you live, your data is kept on our server in Germany: if you play from a country outside the European Union, we receive and keep it there so that you can play. We process the data of all players with the same rules and the same safeguards of the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) described on this page. If the law of the country where you live gives you additional rights over your data, you can exercise them by writing to the same privacy address.

How long we keep it

  • Account and game stay until you delete the account, except abandoned guest games (see the next item). Deleting the account also deletes your public profile, your Mail, the chat messages you wrote or received (including the announcements of your summons), Arena points, damage to Bosses, blocks and accepted rules.
  • As a guest, account and game stay on the server, but the code to get back in is only on the phone: if you uninstall the game, clear its data, reset the phone or change phone without having linked Google, you can no longer get back in. As a guest, logging out deletes the game, like “Delete account”. A guest game without a linked Google account and without sign-ins for 12 months deletes itself, in the same way as “Delete account”.
  • Mail messages stay until the recipient deletes them. The log of sent messages (without the text) is deleted after 2 days.
  • Messages in the global chat and in private chats, including the summon announcements, are deleted from the server after 30 days.
  • The messages you wrote to other players stay in their Mail until they delete them, but without your name: in place of the sender a label says that the player is no longer in the game.
  • Reports stay until we check them; after the decision they are deleted within 30 days. The log of moderation actions is deleted after 30 days; a time-limited action (messages suspended, account suspended) stays until it expires. Reports and actions concerning an account are deleted when that account is deleted; the reports you made about others stay until they expire, without the link to you.
  • When an account’s messages or the account itself are suspended, the block also applies to the phones from which that account has played recently or plays while the block is in force, for anyone playing on those phones, even with an existing account. For each phone we keep the phone fingerprint, the type of block, the reason, the expiry and the date, even if the account is deleted (without the link to the account any more). This data is deleted when the block expires or is lifted; with a permanent suspension it stays as long as the game is active.
  • The Arena battle log is deleted after 30 days. Shared Bosses that have ended, with the participants’ damage, are deleted after 30 days.
  • Closed sessions, with the phone fingerprint and the result of the Google Play check, are deleted after 30 days; a session inactive for 180 days expires.
  • Database backups are made every hour on the same server: hourly copies are kept for 2 days, one copy a day is kept for 30 days. After the account is deleted, your data also disappears from the backups within 30 days.
  • For purchases, after the account is deleted only an irreversible fingerprint of the purchase code remains, with no link to you, to prevent the same purchase from being redeemed twice. The fingerprint stays as long as the game is active. We do not keep players’ tax data: payments are collected by Google or Apple, which issue the receipts.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask to see, correct or delete your data, restrict its use or object to it, and receive a copy. You can delete the account yourself in the game (see Deleting your account) or write to privacy@neobytestudios.com. As a guest we do not have your email: you delete the account from the game. You can also lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, garanteprivacy.it) or with the data protection authority of the country where you live.

Children

The game is intended for people aged 16 or over, because it contains purchases with real money and random items that can be obtained with gems. We do not ask for your date of birth: anyone under 16 must not create an account, not even as a guest. The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13, as also required by the United States law on children’s online privacy (COPPA). If we find out that an account belongs to someone under 16, we delete it with all its data. A parent can ask for its deletion by writing to privacy@neobytestudios.com.

Changes

If the way we use data changes, we update this page and the date at the top.
