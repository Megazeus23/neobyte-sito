The world

The world

The Eclipse Dragon has cast its Veil over six cities. The Guardian is waiting for you at the Bridge: gather your cards and free them, one by one.

Under the Veil the cities have gone dark, and those who lived there have forgotten the dawn. Their guardians now fight for the Dragon. It is up to you to bring back the light.

Sigilli dell’Eclisse is a collectible fantasy card game: you win before the clash, by choosing your cards and their order, then the battle plays out on its own.

What awaits you

  1. 01

    Summon and evolve

    Summon new cards with summoning stones, across six rarities from Common to Legendary. Merge another copy of the same card to evolve it: the artwork changes with every form, up to the fourth.

  2. 02

    Build your squads

    Every card has an ability that triggers on its own in battle: strikes, poisons, shields, heals, even cards that raise a fallen squad. 31 combos between specific cards, plus a bonus for cards of the same realm.

  3. 03

    Free the cities

    Explore 42 districts and face guardians and bosses: with every city you free the Veil grows thinner, all the way to the Dragon’s gate.

  4. 04

    Fight in the Arena, bring down bosses

    From level 30, every week you challenge the players ranked closest to you in the Arena. Bring down a shared Boss together with other players within an hour: if it falls, its card goes to whoever dealt the most damage.
