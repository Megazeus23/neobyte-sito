Sigilli dell’Eclisse is on course for Google Play

World 01 on our map is getting closer: a fantasy card game with 78 cards, each in four forms, coming to Android.

World 01 on our map is almost in sight. Sigilli dell’Eclisse is coming to Google Play, for Android phones. The release date has not been announced yet: we will write it here as soon as there is one.

The Eclipse Dragon has cast its Veil over six cities. The Guardian is waiting for you at the Bridge: gather your cards and free them, one by one.

There are 78 cards to collect, each in four forms, 42 districts to explore and 31 card combos. You prepare your victory before the fight, choosing the cards and their order; then the battle plays out on its own.

On the game page you will find the screenshots, the cards and all the details. When the store goes live, the link will appear there.
