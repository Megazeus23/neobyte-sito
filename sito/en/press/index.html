Sigilli dell’Eclisse

Description

Free six cities from the Veil of the Eclipse with fantasy cards that evolve.

The Eclipse Dragon has cast its Veil over six cities. The Guardian is waiting for you at the Bridge: gather your cards and free them, one by one.

Under the Veil the cities have gone dark, and those who lived there have forgotten the dawn. Their guardians now fight for the Dragon. It is up to you to bring back the light.

Sigilli dell’Eclisse is a collectible fantasy card game: you win before the clash, by choosing your cards and their order, then the battle plays out on its own.
