01Support

Need a hand? We are listening.

Answers to the most common questions. If yours is not here, write to us.

General questions

Where can I find NeoByte Studios games?

Only on the official stores. Each game page has the links; if a store is not live yet, it is marked as “Soon on…”.

Open the world map

Which devices are your games for?

It depends on the game. The platforms are listed on its page, under “Details”.

I have a problem with a game: who do I write to?

Write to support@neobytestudios.com. Tell us the game, the device you use and what happened: we answer in person.

Write to support

How do I ask to delete my account or my data?

Each game has its own account deletion page: you will find it on the game page, under “Game documents”. For any question about personal data, write to privacy@neobytestudios.com.

Does this site use cookies?

No. This site uses no cookies and no tracking tools. Your preferences, such as ambient sound on or off, stay on your device only.

I write or make videos about your games: where do I find logos and images?

In the press room: logos, images and descriptions, also as a single file to download.

Open the press room

Sigilli dell’Eclisse

When is Sigilli dell’Eclisse coming out?

It is coming to Google Play, for Android phones. The date has not been announced yet: when there is one, you will find it in the Ship’s log and on the game page.

Go to the game page

Do I need the Internet to play?

Yes. An Internet connection is required: your game is saved on the server and you will find it on any phone with the same Google account.

Are there purchases in the game?

The game includes optional purchases with real money, including random items (summoning stones). The odds are shown on the summoning screen, and stones can also be earned by playing.

How do the four card forms work?

Every card has four forms. Merge another copy of the same card to evolve it: the artwork changes with every form, up to the fourth.
